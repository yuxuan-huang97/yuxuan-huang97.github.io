# Introduction

## About me

&emsp;&emsp;I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, advised by Prof. Victoria Interrante. My research interests lie in Virtual Reality, HCI, and Computer Graphics. I am particularly interested in how virtual environments and gamification can be used for social good. My CV can be found [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sgnTONnIVrth2ysC90cf6fhAXdhkTLS-/view?usp=drive_link).\
&emsp;&emsp;In my spare time I love traveling and photography, and from time to time I model 3D digital replicas of places I like in the real world. I also have a passion for art and enjoy designing and doing handcrafts.
